Quick-reference aid. From the AW189 QRH WAT tables, Figures 1-30, 1-32, 1-34, 1-37 and 1-39 (Anti Ice OFF; Heater OFF, or OFF/ON for offshore), interpolated between table values. The published tables remain the reference.

Maximum weight

Enter OAT and pressure altitude
Method: each table is interpolated in a straight line between the two nearest pressure altitudes (500 ft steps) and temperatures (10 °C steps, 50 to 55 °C). If any table value needed is blank, the profile shows "outside the table". The unfactored wind credit (Figures 1-30, 1-32 and 1-39) is interpolated between the listed winds and added; Clear Area and Offshore take-off have no wind table, so no credit is given. Enter the headwind you are allowed to use under your factoring rules. Results are rounded down to the whole kg and never exceed the profile's maximum (8,300 kg, or 8,600 kg with the weight extension where the table allows it).
